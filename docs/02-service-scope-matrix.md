## Boundary placement rule applied

Boundaries sit next to the offering they limit, in the same paragraph or the
one after. There is no single catch-all disclaimer page absorbing all seven.
This follows the contract requirement not to bury the page in generic
disclaimer text, and it matches the brief's Pillar 3 — the limit is volunteered
in the same breath as the capability, not quarantined where it reads as legal
boilerplate.

## Reconciliation with the existing V1 About-page disclaimer

The live `/about` disclaimed contractor, property-management, inspection, legal
and tax services. That is **existing copy, not independent verification of
scope** — it does not prove Jason lacks a credential today, and it is not
evidence that he holds one.

Handling: every one of those five boundaries is preserved in the new copy, but
re-authored in first person and moved next to the relevant service instead of
sitting in one block. Nothing was silently deleted. Nothing new was asserted.

If Jason later confirms a credential in any of the five areas, the specific
boundary is rewritten — and only that one.

drafted copy, and none may be added without the listed confirmation:

- Any price, rent or availability figure. Every figure in the corpus is
  historical; T05 self-dates to March 2024.
- Any outcome, appreciation or yield projection. `/invest` says so explicitly.
